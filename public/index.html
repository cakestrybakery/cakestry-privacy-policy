Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cakestry Bakery Bahawal Nagar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal information when you interact with our WhatsApp customer service chatbot, order management platform, and related business communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you communicate with Cakestry Bakery via WhatsApp or place an order, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and delivery recipient name.
  • Order Details: Cake flavors, weight/size specifications, custom text writing requests, delivery address, date, and preferred time slot.
  • Message Content: Questions, inquiries, and chat history sent to our WhatsApp business account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your bakery orders.
  • To answer customer inquiries about product pricing, availability, and opening hours.
  • To provide order status notifications, receipts, and customer support.
  • To improve our automated AI customer service system powered by Meta Cloud API and OpenAI.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only with essential technology service providers necessary to operate our business:

  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp): For receiving and transmitting WhatsApp messages securely under Meta's global data processing terms.
  • OpenAI API: For processing customer inquiry text to generate natural automated responses. No personal customer identities are used to train external public AI models.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All HTTP communications use industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3).

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your personal data, chat history, and customer records at any time.

To request data deletion, you can:

  • Send a WhatsApp message containing "DELETE MY DATA" to our official business number +92 321 6759463.
  • Or visit our dedicated User Data Deletion Request Page.

Upon receiving your request, all personal record identifiers associated with your phone number will be permanently deleted from our active database within 48 hours.
