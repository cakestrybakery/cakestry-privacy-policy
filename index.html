Privacy Policy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cakestry Bakery Bahawal Nagar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects customer data collected through our official WhatsApp customer service chatbot, order management system, and digital communication platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Cakestry Bakery via WhatsApp, we collect only the necessary information required to fulfill your orders and answer inquiries:

  • Contact Details: Your mobile phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and recipient contact info.
  • Bakery Order Information: Selected cake flavors, weight/poundage (e.g. 2-pound, 3-pound), custom written text, delivery recipient address, date, and preferred delivery time.
  • Communication History: Incoming customer messages sent to our Meta WhatsApp Business account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate bakery business operations:

  • To process, confirm, and deliver bakery and custom cake orders.
  • To send automated WhatsApp order status updates and receipts.
  • To respond to customer questions regarding pricing, product availability, and delivery areas in Bahawal Nagar.

3. Third-Party Data Processing & Integrations

We respect your privacy and never sell, lease, or distribute customer data to third parties. We share data only with authorized technology providers essential for service delivery:

  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp): Transmits encrypted WhatsApp messages under Meta’s global developer terms.
  • OpenAI API: Processes customer inquiry text to generate instant natural language responses. Customer data is not retained to train external public models.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data transmitted between our servers and third-party APIs uses industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3) encryption. Database records are stored securely with restricted administrative access.

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

Customers have full control over their data and may request total deletion of their customer profile, order history, and chat logs at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion:
  1. Send a WhatsApp message containing "DELETE MY DATA" to our registered business number +92 321 6759463.
  2. Or email your phone number deletion request to privacy@cakestry.com.

Upon receipt of your request, all personal record identifiers will be permanently removed from our active database within 48 hours.
